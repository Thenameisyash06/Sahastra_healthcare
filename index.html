Integrated Healthcare

Two Essential Specialties, One Trusted Clinic

SAHASTRA OCUDENT HEALTHCARE is an integrated medical center uniting comprehensive eye care and complete dental healthcare. We are dedicated to providing ethical, accessible, and patient-focused consultations in a modern clinical setting.

Eye Care & Ophthalmology

From precision diagnostic vision screenings and optical corrections to advanced surgical solutions including Phacoemulsification and SICS for cataract management, our eye care department provides thorough clinical assessments to protect and enhance your sight.

Dental Care & Orthodontics

Our dental wing covers preventive cleanings, gentle endodontic root canal therapies, modern braces and aligners, restorative dental implants, and compassionate pediatric oral healthcare for children in a calm, welcoming environment.
