About Sahastra Eye Care

Excellence in Micro-Surgical Ophthalmology

Dedicated to preserving and restoring visual clarity through high-precision diagnostics, sutureless micro-incision cataract surgery, and compassionate, evidence-based ophthalmic care in Wakad, Pune.

About Sahastra Dental Care

Excellence in Modern & Painless Dentistry

Dedicated to providing gentle, anxiety-free oral healthcare, conservative restorative dentistry, and beautiful, confident smiles through state-of-the-art dental technology.

Ophthalmic Foundation

Clinical Rigor & Evidence-Based Eye Care

Sight is our most cherished sensory connection to the world. At Sahastra Eye Care, our practice is founded on absolute diagnostic integrity, conservative therapy, and microsurgical precision.

Diagnostic Accuracy

High-resolution digital slit-lamp biomicroscopy and applanation tonometry ensure exact ocular pressure and anterior segment mapping.

Micro-Incision Phaco

Sutureless 2.2mm cataract procedures with ultrasonic precision and customized intraocular lens (IOL) selection for rapid healing.

Conservative Ethics

We never push unwarranted surgeries or procedures. Every finding is photographed and transparently explained to you.

Frictionless OPD Scheduling

Schedule comprehensive vision screenings online or via WhatsApp with minimal waiting room delays and dedicated follow-up.

Dental Foundation

Painless Dental Science & Patient Comfort

We are dedicated to shattering the myth that dental care has to be painful or intimidating. Our operatory combines gentle chairside touch with computerized micro-endodontics.

Pain-Free Techniques

Single-sitting rotary endodontics with precision apex locators and gentle topical anesthesia ensure complete patient comfort.

Conservative Tooth Preservation

We prioritize saving your natural tooth structure with biomimetic ceramic restorations rather than rushing into extractions.

Hospital-Grade Sterility

Class-B vacuum autoclaving, individual sealed pouches, and anti-retraction handpieces guarantee zero cross-contamination.

Transparent Itemized Estimates

Detailed treatment plans with transparent pricing, zero unexpected post-treatment charges, and honest material specifications.

Precision Infrastructure

Ophthalmic Diagnostic & Surgical Equipment

Micro-surgical ophthalmology demands uncompromising optical clarity. Our facility is equipped with global gold-standard instrumentation.

Carl Zeiss Lumera Microscope

Red-reflex surgical illumination providing brilliant stereoscopic depth during delicate capsulorhexis and lens alignment.

Alcon Infiniti Phaco System

Advanced fluidics and torsional ultrasound handpiece ensuring minimal thermal impact and crystal-clear post-operative corneas.

Nidek Digital Slit-Lamp

High-definition digital camera attachment capturing anterior chamber cross-sections for immediate patient visualization.

Goldmann Applanation Tonometry

Internationally recognized gold standard for precise intraocular pressure measurement and early glaucoma detection.

Operatory Infrastructure

Advanced Dental Operatory Equipment

Modern conservative dentistry relies on digital imaging and precision rotary endodontics. Explore our clinical technology suite.

Dentsply Sirona Rotary Motor

Computer-controlled torque and reciprocating motion preventing file separation and achieving painless root canal sanitization.

Vatech Digital RVG Sensor

Ultra-low radiation digital radiovisiography delivering instantaneous chairside X-ray imaging with microscopic bone definition.

Euronda Class-B Vacuum Autoclave

Fractionated pre-vacuum thermal steam sterilization meeting European EN 13060 standards for 100% spore destruction.

Woodpecker Piezo Scaler

Gentle ultrasonic periodontal scaling technology that removes subgingival calculus without scratching natural dental enamel.
