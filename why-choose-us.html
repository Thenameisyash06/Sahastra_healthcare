Infection Prevention & Hygiene

Ophthalmic Micro-Surgical Sterility Protocols

Patient safety is our uncompromising foundation. Every micro-surgical eye instrument undergoes multi-barrier disinfection exceeding European EN 13060 standards.

Step 01

Ultrasonic Decontamination

Pre-cleaning in automated medical-grade ultrasonic baths utilizing enzymatic detergents to dislodge micro-debris and completely dissolve biological residue prior to thermal processing.

Step 02

Class-B Vacuum Autoclaving

Fractionated triple-pulsed vacuum cycles extract all trapped air, ensuring 100% steam penetration into hollow instruments, cannulas, and surgical handpieces at 134°C under 2.1 bar pressure.

Step 03

Pouching & Chemical Tracking

Instruments are hermetically sealed in medical-grade pouches with dual internal & external Class 5 multi-parameter chemical integrators that verify cycle success before opening in front of you.

Step 04

Purified Ophthalmic Air Purity

Operating suites utilize continuous positive pressure HEPA 14 air filtration, preventing aerosol particulates, paired with scheduled terminal UV-C room surface sterilization.

Infection Prevention & Hygiene

Dental Operatory Cross-Infection Control

Patient safety is our uncompromising foundation. Every dental handpiece and operatory undergoes multi-barrier disinfection exceeding European EN 13060 standards.

Step 01

Ultrasonic Decontamination

Rotary burrs and endodontic instruments are submerged in ultrasonic cavitation baths with enzymatic detergents to dissolve bio-burden before thermal processing.

Step 02

Class-B Vacuum Autoclaving

High-temperature fractional steam sterilization (134°C, 2.1 bar) penetrating the complex internal hollow lumens of dental handpieces and rotary heads (EN 13060 standard).

Step 03

Individual Sealed Pouches

Instruments are hermetically heat-sealed with multi-parameter chemical indicators that confirm sterility before they are unsealed chairside in front of you.

Step 04

Closed Waterline Sanitization

Closed-circuit medical water reservoirs prevent biofilm development in dental lines, paired with continuous operatory air purification and anti-retraction valves.
