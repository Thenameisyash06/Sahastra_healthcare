Ophthalmic Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions — Eye Care

Honest answers to the most common questions our cataract, glaucoma, and vision patients ask.

How long does Phaco cataract surgery take, and is it painful?

The procedure takes only 15 to 20 minutes under topical anesthetic eye drops. You remain awake and comfortable without needles, stitches, or eye patches. Most patients resume gentle daily activities within 24 to 48 hours.

What is the difference between Monofocal, Toric, and Multifocal IOLs?

A monofocal lens provides sharp distance vision, requiring reading glasses for near tasks. Toric lenses correct pre-existing corneal astigmatism. Premium multifocal or trifocal lenses restore vision at multiple distances (distance, intermediate screen, and reading). Dr. Karansing Dhanraj Chavan assesses your lifestyle before recommending the ideal option.

Why is early glaucoma detection called "the silent sight thief"?

Because open-angle glaucoma causes zero pain and gradual peripheral vision loss that your brain compensates for. By the time you notice vision changes, irreversible optic nerve damage has occurred. Annual tonometry checks are crucial for adults over 40.

Do you accept cashless health insurance for cataract surgery?

Yes. Sahastra Ocudent Healthcare assists with cashless insurance and TPA pre-authorization for all major health insurance providers. Our front desk provides itemized estimates with complete transparency and zero hidden consumable fees.

Dental Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Care

Honest answers to the most common questions our root canal, implant, and aligner patients ask.

Is single-sitting rotary root canal treatment really painless?

Yes! With modern computerized local anesthesia and motorized nickel-titanium rotary files, the actual procedure is completely pain-free. Patients feel immediate relief from the throbbing ache caused by the underlying infected tooth pulp.

How long do dental implants last, and what is the success rate?

Dental implants boast a clinical success rate of over 97%. Because the biocompatible titanium post fuses directly with your jawbone (osseointegration), an implant can easily last a lifetime with routine oral hygiene and 6-month checkups.

Are clear aligners suitable for all types of crooked teeth?

Clear aligners effectively correct mild to moderately severe crowding, gaps, rotations, and minor bite misalignments. During your 3D digital scan, Dr. Aditi Rajesh Pawar generates a complete treatment preview showing the exact movements and duration before you begin.

When should my child visit the dentist for the first time?

We recommend their first dental checkup either when their first milk tooth erupts or around their first birthday. Early visits acclimate children positively to the dental chair and allow us to apply painless fluoride varnish and fissure sealants.
